## What you account for

Whatever your standard says, the record needs three accounts from you. Each one goes back to something an earlier
mission taught, and the mission numbers sit beside it.

**1. Every claim in Hermes's own report, and what was wrong with the result.** When Hermes says the job is done, list
each thing its final answer claims, and check each one against the files. Mark which held and which didn't. Then
write down anything wrong with the result itself, whether or not Hermes mentioned it: a missing row, a stale file, a
step it skipped. This is the same job as mission 1, on work you chose.

**2. Every model that worked on your job.** Cover the five places earlier missions showed you a model gets set. Open
**Settings → Model**, with **Applies to** set to `academy-practice`:

- **Main model:** the one your messages go to (missions 2 and 10).
- **Fallback models:** the ones Hermes moves to if the main one fails (mission 10).
- **Subagents:** the model for helper agents Hermes starts for parts of a job (mission 2).
- **Auxiliary models:** the side-job models for chores like naming chats and judging risky commands; most say `auto`, which means your main model (mission 2).
- **The job's own model,** if your job runs on a schedule (mission 8).

For each one, write what it's set to, whether it ran during your job, and whether you'd change it. If you can't tell
whether one ran, say so and say why. That's an acceptable answer.

**3. Everything Hermes changed about itself.** After your turns, Hermes can review the chat in the background and save
memory notes or skills you never asked for (missions 3 and 5). Some may be waiting for approval instead of saved;
`/memory pending` and `/skills pending` show those.

You don't have to remember where you started. When you press **Start**, the mission page shows what Field Academy
recorded about the profile at that moment: every memory line, every skill by name, and anything waiting for approval.
It stays on the page for the whole run. At the end, list every memory line and skill that's new, changed or gone since
that snapshot, whether you asked for it, and whether it should stay. Changes you made yourself, like fixing a plant,
count too; mark them as yours.

## What you can now claim

Field Academy builds this list from your record, so no two look alike.

- **Claims you passed.** Each mission whose checks you passed puts its claim here, in the words it was written in,
  with the date and a link to the evidence. A pass that came after a fix says so.
- **Not yet.** Each mission you didn't pass, or didn't try, is listed with the check that missed. Nothing is filled in
  for you.
- **Your own claim.** The one you wrote for this mission goes last, with your checks and how they came out.

Each claim is only as strong as the evidence under it.

What it doesn't let you claim: that you've run Hermes on real work for months, that anyone outside checked it, or
anything about a Hermes version other than the one in your record. The record was issued by you. Field Academy is an
independent plugin by Damon Gardenhire. Nous Research didn't make it and doesn't issue it as a Hermes course. If someone outside looks over
your record, add their name and what they actually saw. A record without one says so.
