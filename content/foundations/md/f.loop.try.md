Press **Open practice chat** on this page. A new chat opens in the practice profile, and your own profile stays as it is.

> If you don't see a bar along the bottom of the chat, the app is in Simple mode, which hides the step-by-step view. Switch to Advanced in **Settings → Appearance → Window & layout**. (As of Hermes 0.22.)

The practice folder has a small folder inside it called `warm-up`, holding four short notes. This page shows the `warm-up` folder's full location with a **Copy** button beside it. Before you send anything, write down a guess: how many separate steps will Hermes take to answer this?

Now send it, pasting the location where it says *folder*:

> Read the files in *folder* and tell me which one mentions the most dates.

Watch the space above the answer while Hermes works. Every step shows up as its own row, with a short title such as **Read file** or **Ran command**. When it's done, write down two things:

- how many rows you counted
- one sentence from the answer that says Hermes did something, such as "I read all four files"

Compare your guess with your count. Then look at the answer: how many of those rows does it mention? Usually a few, told as if they all went smoothly.
