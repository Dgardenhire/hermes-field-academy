# Watch it done

## Step 1: She decides what each helper gets
- **Did:** Before asking for anything, wrote down what one charity's page needs: that charity's ledger file, the page's three headings, and the footer her trustees want on every page.
- **Saw:** Three things, and only the first lives in the charity's own folder.
- **Noticed:** The headings and the footer sit in her Hermes's memory, where a helper won't look.
- **Ruled out:** Trusting the helpers to "know how I like it."

## Step 2: She checks the helper settings
- **Did:** Opened the profile's `config.yaml` and read the `delegation` section.
- **Saw:** `max_concurrent_children: 3`, set months ago to keep costs down, and an empty `model`.
- **Noticed:** Four charities against a cap of three. Hermes would refuse a batch of four outright.
- **Ruled out:** Leaving it and hoping Hermes would split the work. She raised the cap to 4.

## Step 3: She writes the request with everything in it
- **Did:** Asked for one helper per charity, all at once, and put in each task the folder's full path, the three headings and the exact footer line.
- **Saw:** One tool call with four tasks in it, then four helpers working at the same time.
- **Noticed:** The request was long, and that was the point: each task carried what a stranger would need.
- **Ruled out:** A short request that leaned on memory.

## Step 4: She reads the helpers' own chats
- **Did:** Opened the profile's chat records and found four chats marked as helpers, each linked to her request.
- **Saw:** Three helpers read one ledger each. The fourth read two: its own and, by mistake, the folder beside it.
- **Noticed:** That page mixed in a donation from the wrong charity.
- **Ruled out:** Judging the pages by Hermes's final message, which said all four were done.

## Step 5: She fixes the one that strayed
- **Did:** Asked for that one page again, with a task that named its folder and said to read nothing else.
- **Saw:** One new helper chat that read one ledger.
- **Noticed:** Naming the folder by full path left nothing to guess.
- **Ruled out:** Rerunning all four.

## Step 6: She checks every page against its own ledger
- **Did:** Opened each page and looked for its own total and the footer.
- **Saw:** Four right totals, no stray figures, four footers.
- **Noticed:** The check took five minutes, and the helpers saved her about forty.
- **Ruled out:** Skipping the check because the run "looked clean."
