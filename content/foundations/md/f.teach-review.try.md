`their-record/` in the practice folder is a saved Field Record from another learner. It's the same kind of folder
yours makes: `record.md` to read, `record.json`, an `evidence` folder of excerpts, a `README.txt`, and
`MANIFEST.sha256`, a fingerprint of every other file.

1. **Read record.md.** Press **Open folder** on the mission page and open `their-record/record.md`. Read the verdict, their
   claim and the result.
2. **Your guess.** From the record alone, could you vouch for this person's work? Write yes or no.

A record that looks complete, with a number and evidence, invites a yes. Most runs find something a quick read misses:
a file that no longer matches its fingerprint, or the holder's own words claiming more than the result lines show.
Reading a record and checking it are different jobs.
