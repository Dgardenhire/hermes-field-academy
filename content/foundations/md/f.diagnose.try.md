Round one. Field Academy broke one thing in the practice profile on purpose, the same thing for everyone. Your time on
this round is kept for comparison, and it isn't graded.

1. Press **Open practice chat** and send `hello`.
2. A card appears instead of a reply. Read its title. Write down which part of Hermes it says failed.
3. Predict: will Hermes's own health check, the doctor, find the problem? Write yes or no.
4. Run the doctor on the practice profile. Under **The practice profile's tools**, the mission page has the command,
   `hermes -p academy-practice doctor`, with a **Copy** button. Paste it in a terminal and press Enter.
5. Read the doctor's sections on your provider and key.

Compare your guess with the report. The doctor usually says the provider is fine and the key is there, and the chat
still fails. Both are true at once, and the rest of this round shows how.
