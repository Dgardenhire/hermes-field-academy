# Watch it done

## Step 1: She writes a full request
- **Did:** Wrote "Read the five daily job sheets in this week's folder. Write one summary to `weekly-summary.md` in the same folder with the number of jobs per day, total hours and any job marked unpaid."
- **Saw:** A request that names the folder, the files and where the output goes.
- **Noticed:** The more she names, the less Hermes has to guess.
- **Ruled out:** A loose "summarize this week's jobs."

## Step 2: She watches the rows
- **Did:** Watched the rows while Hermes worked.
- **Saw:** Five reads appeared at once. Then a search she didn't ask for, in the archive folder.
- **Noticed:** Old sheets would muddy the totals.
- **Ruled out:** Waiting for the answer before looking.

## Step 3: She steers
- **Did:** Typed `/steer only use the five files in this week's folder`.
- **Saw:** The search finished, her note arrived, and the next step went back to the right folder.
- **Noticed:** She didn't stop the turn, so the five reads weren't lost.
- **Ruled out:** Pressing Stop and starting over.

## Step 4: She lists the claims before believing any
- **Did:** Wrote down every claim in the summary: "Read all five sheets. 38 jobs, Monday to Friday. 112 hours. Two unpaid. Saved to weekly-summary.md."
- **Saw:** Five claims.
- **Noticed:** Each one is something she can check.
- **Ruled out:** Reading the summary as a whole and nodding along.

## Step 5: She starts with the count
- **Did:** Checked the 38 against the sheets, one day at a time.
- **Saw:** 38 is about what a normal week runs.
- **Noticed:** That's why she doesn't trust it. A wrong total that looks normal is the one the owner will never question.
- **Ruled out:** Starting with the claims that would be easy to spot if wrong.

## Step 6: She finds the hole
- **Did:** Expanded Thursday's read row.
- **Saw:** Thursday's sheet is a header row and nothing else; the office tablet's export had failed. The read has a check mark, and it came back with one line.
- **Noticed:** The 38 came from four days.
- **Ruled out:** Trusting the check mark on the read.

## Step 7: She checks the rest, then fixes the cause
- **Did:** Checked the file exists, its date is from a minute ago, and the two unpaid jobs match the sheets. Re-exported Thursday and ran the request again in a new chat.
- **Saw:** A new total, which she checked against all five sheets.
- **Noticed:** A rerun on the same bad input would have given the same wrong answer.
- **Ruled out:** Rerunning before fixing the cause.
