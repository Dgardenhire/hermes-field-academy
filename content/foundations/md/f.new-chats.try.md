`SOUL.md` is a short file that tells Hermes who it is and how to talk. Each profile has its own.

1. Press **Open practice chat** on this page to start a chat in `academy-practice`. Ask: `Suggest a name for a goldfish.` Leave this chat open in its tab.
2. At the foot of the sidebar there's a row of small squares, one per profile. Hover a square to see its name. Find `academy-practice`, right-click its square and choose **Edit SOUL.md…** That opens the file without switching profiles. A left-click switches the whole app to that profile. If you land in the wrong profile, click your own square and the Field Academy page comes back.
3. Add this on its own line, then click **Save SOUL.md**: `End every reply with the words: Over and out.`
4. Predict: will the chat you left open start signing off "Over and out"? Write yes or no.
5. Go back to that chat and ask: `And a name for a turtle?`
6. Press **Open practice chat** again for a fresh chat, and ask the turtle question there.

Look at both replies. The chat you left open ignores your rule, and the fresh one follows it, though they share a profile and a file and started a minute apart.

When you're done, take the line back out of `SOUL.md` and save, so it doesn't follow you into the rest of the mission.
