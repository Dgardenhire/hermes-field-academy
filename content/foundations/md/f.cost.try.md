Press **Open practice chat** on this page. A new chat opens in `academy-practice`, the practice profile Field Academy set up for you. Type one word: `hello`. Send it.

Now look at the bar along the bottom of the chat. There's a meter showing how full the chat is. Click it. A panel opens called **Context Usage**, splitting the chat into parts, such as **System prompt** and **Tool definitions**. A part only shows when there's something in it.

> If you don't see a bar along the bottom, the app is in Simple mode. Switch to Advanced in **Settings → Appearance → Window & layout**. (As of Hermes 0.22.)

Before you read on, write down two numbers:

- the total the panel shows for this chat
- how much of it is the word you typed

A one-word message ought to be cheap. Look at your total anyway. In a typical setup it's several thousand tokens, sometimes tens of thousands, and your hello is two or three of them.
