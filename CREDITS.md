## The learning research

The design of reviews, worked examples, feedback, placement and the capstone follows published research by Roediger and Karpicke, Cepeda and others, Sweller, Renkl and Atkinson, Chi, Hattie and Timperley, Kalyuga, Barnett and Ceci, and others. docs/pedagogy.md names each paper, says which numbers are Field Academy's own choices, and says which claims could not be verified.
